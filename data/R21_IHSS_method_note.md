# IHSS soil humic and fulvic acid method

Official page: https://humic-substances.org/isolation-of-ihss-soil-fulvic-and-humic-acids/

The method separates humic acid precipitate from the fulvic acid supernatant by acidifying an alkaline soil extract to about pH 1 and allowing 12–16 h before centrifugation. This supports a low-pH humic-acid-precipitation *possibility*. It does not measure how much HA precipitates in the proposal's short, pH 2–4, 10 mL batch extraction. The original webpage is attributed to Swift (1996) and reproduced by IHSS with permission; only the relevant short note and URL are retained here rather than redistributing its complete text.
